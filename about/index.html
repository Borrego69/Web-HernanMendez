About

Who Is Hernán

Hernán Méndez is a photographer, cinematographer, camera operator, field sound recorder, and animator based in Tempe, Arizona. Born in Mexicali, Baja California, he grew up between the desert and the Sea of Cortez, spending weekends in San Felipe, where his family operates an RV campground. Those early years—reading tides, navigating pangas (small fishing boats), watching how light shifted across water and sand—shaped the observational discipline that defines his work today.

He earned a full athletic scholarship to play football at Tecnológico de Monterrey, where he studied Industrial Design. Photography first captured his attention in high school, and he developed it further through product photography and 3D modeling in college. Those foundations in form, proportion, material, and light still inform his approach across still and motion work.

Long before opening his own studio, Hernán worked at TM Advertising in Houston, contributing to national broadcast, digital, and print campaigns for clients such as American Airlines, Nationwide Insurance, Texas Tourism, Supercuts, Baylor Health, and Hotels.com. He supported directors of photography on commercial sets, assisted with AC work and camera prep, captured BTS and secondary plates, and maintained calibrated color pipelines across motion and print. The work exposed him to large-scale production, fast-moving creative teams, and the technical standards of agency-level commercial work.

He later moved into documentary and nonfiction film as a cinematographer and assistant producer with Elicosma Production, shooting interviews, b-roll, and location footage in multiple countries. He coordinated international travel, trained scouting teams, secured access to archaeological and cultural sites, supported underwater and cave-diving shoots, and conducted research that informed narrative development. That field work strengthened his logistical discipline and taught him to keep technical precision in unpredictable environments.

In 2018, Hernán founded Arrecife Photography, a studio specializing in commercial, product, and architectural imaging, as well as 360° tours, video production, and audio recording. He completed his M.S. in Graphic Information Technology at Arizona State University, focusing on commercial photography, video and audio workflows, 360° capture, media production pipelines, and the business side of creative work. His graduate studies also included stop-motion animation, podcast production, and film, reflecting a multidisciplinary practice grounded in design thinking and technical rigor.
